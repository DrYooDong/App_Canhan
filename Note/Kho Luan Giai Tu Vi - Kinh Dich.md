## 1. NEN TANG TRIET HOC: SYNCHRONICITY & KINH DICH

### 1.1. Nguyen Ly Synchronicity cua C.G. Jung

Synchronicity (Dong bo phi nhan qua) la khai niem Jung phat trien lan dau nam 1930 khi viet dieu van ve Richard Wilhelm – dich gia Kinh Dich. Ong dung no de giai thich co che van hanh (modus operandi) cua Kinh Dich.

DINH NGHIA: Synchronicity la su trung hop co y nghia giua hai su kien khong co lien he nhan qua, nhung xay ra dong thoi va mang nghia chung.

Ung dung vao Tu Vi & Kinh Dich:
- Gio/ngay/thang/nam sinh khong GAY RA van menh, nhung PHAN ANH cung mot mau hinh vu tru tai thoi diem do
- Boi Kinh Dich khong DOAN tuong lai, ma KHOP voi trang thai noi tam va hoan canh khach quan dong thoi
- Su kien xay ra khi "canh cua vo thuc mo ra" - khi cam xuc manh, khi archetyp (nguyen mau) bi kich hoat

### 1.2. Kinh Dich trong Mat Jung

Jung danh gia Kinh Dich la he thong mantology (thuat boi toan) tinh vi nhat:
- Co che: Tao dieu kien cho synchronistic phenomena xuat hien
- Tam ly: Kich hoat vo thuc tap the, archetyp noi len
- Xac suat: Ket qua nam trong gioi han xac suat nhung "meaningful"
- Muc dich: Khong doan tuong lai co dinh ma DOC HIEN TAI SAU

THI NGHIEM cua Jung (1952): Phan tich 483 cap la so hon nhan. Ket qua 3 lan lien tiep khop voi truyen thong chiem tinh co dien voi xac suat 1:62,500,000. Ket luan: Khong phai ngau nhien thuan tuy, khong phai nhan qua -> Synchronistic phenomenon.

### 1.3. Tu Tuong Dong Nhat Dong-Tay

| Khai niem | Dong Phuong | Jung/Tay Phuong |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Van vat tuong lien | Am Duong bien hoa | Pre-established harmony (Leibniz) |
| Y nghia tiem an | Thien co, menh so | Acausal orderedness |
| Truc giac phi tuyen | Boi que, la so | Mantic procedures |
| Con nguoi-vu tru | Vi nhan = dai vu tru | Microcosm-macrocosm |
| Thoi gian co chat | Gio sinh quyet dinh | Psychic relativity of time |

Pythagore: "Nguyen thuy cua van vat la mot the duy nhat, tu do bien ra so..."
Dong Trong Thu (Han): "Troi dat hop lai la mot, chia ra la am va duong, phan ra bon mua, sap xep theo ngu hanh."

## 3. 14 CHINH TINH - DAC TINH & LUAN GIAI

### 3.1. TU VI (De Tinh - Duong Tho)
Chu: Uy quyen, tai loc, phuc duc

Vi tri Mieu dia: Ty, Ngo, Dan, Than -> Thong minh, trung hau, uy nghi, ruc ro nhat
Vi tri Vuong dia: Thin, Tuat -> Thong minh, da muu tuc tri, co oai phong
Vi tri Dac dia: Suu, Mui -> Thong minh, thao luoc, doi khi lieu linh
Vi tri Binh hoa: Hoi, Ty, Mao, Dau -> Kem ruc ro, giam uy luc

Tinh cach: Mat do, lung day, trung hau, uy nghi. Sao chu te, giang phuc cho bat cu cung nao no tru ngu.

Phoi hop quan trong:
- Gap Phu -> than giu kho, cua cai suc tich
- Gap Sat (That Sat) -> vua mang kiem bau, tang uy quyen
- Gap Tuong, Xuong, Khuc, Khoi, Viet, Ta, Huu -> quan than khanh hoi, phu quy hien hach
- Gap Hoa, Linh -> Tu Vi che duoc hung ac
- KY NHAT: Tuan, Triet an ngu + Khong, Kiep -> vua bi vay ham, suot doi trac tro

NHAN SINH LUAN (Jung): Tu Vi bieu tuong cho ban nga trung tam (The Self archetype) - dieu phoi toan bo la so nhu Hoang de dieu phoi trieu dinh.

---

### 3.2. LIEM TRINH (Am Hoa - Tu Tinh)
Chu: Quan loc, hinh nguc

Mieu (Thin, Tuat): Liem khiet, thang than, nghiem nghi
Vuong (Ty, Ngo, Dan, Than): Can dam, dung manh
Ham (Ti, Hoi, Mao, Dau): Khac nghiet, gian hiem, hay vuong tu toi

Dac biet: Liem Mieu/Vuong/Dac co them dac tinh Dao Hoa (hap dan). Liem ham + nhieu Sat tinh -> bo sao tac hoa khung khiep.

---

### 3.3. THIEN DONG (Duong Thuy - Phuc Tinh)
Chu: Phuc tho

Mieu (Dan, Than): Phuc tho song toan, hoa nha
Vuong (Ty): Dac biet tot
Ham (Ngo, Dau, Thin, Tuat, Suu, Mui): Tai hoa, thi phi, kien cao

Dac tinh: Canh cai, that thuong, nhan hau. Tuoi Dinh gap Thien Dong o cung ham -> lai thanh tot dep (nghich ly may man).

---

### 3.4. VU KHUC (Am Kim - Tai Tinh)
Chu: Tai loc

Mieu (Thin, Tuat, Suu, Mui): Cuong nghi, dung manh, tang tai tien loc
Vuong (Dan, Than, Ty, Ngo): Kinh doanh gioi
Dac (Mao, Dau): Kha nhung de benh tat
Ham (Ti, Hoi): Tham lam, uong nganh, pha tan

Co doc: Vu Khuc co tinh chat Co Qua -> hay khac nguoi than, khac vo/chong, hiem con. Phai ngoai 30 tuoi moi phat dat (Suu, Mui).

---

### 3.5. THAI DUONG (Duong Hoa - Quy Tinh)
Chu: Quan loc
Bieu tuong: Mat troi, nguoi cha, ong noi. Doi voi dan ba con la bieu tuong cua chong.

Mieu (Ti, Ngo): Ruc ro nhat - nhu mat troi giua trua
Vuong (Dan, Mao, Thin): Dang len, tot dep
Dac (Suu, Mui): Kha tot
Ham (Than, Dau, Tuat, Hoi, Ty): Giam uy quyen, kem thong minh

Quy tac dac biet:
- Sinh ban ngay + Duong Nam/Nu -> tang ve ruc ro
- Gap Hoa Ky dong cung o Suu/Mui -> toan my
- Ham dia gap Tuan/Triet -> lai thanh sang sua

---

### 3.6. THIEN CO (Am Moc - Thien Tinh)
Chu: Huynh de, phuc tho

Mieu (Thin, Tuat, Mao, Dau): Sang suot, kheo leo, co muu co, nhan hau
Vuong (Ti, Than): Kha tot
Ham (Dan, Hoi): Kem sang suot

Cap sao: Thien Co + Thien Luong o Ngo, Thin, Tuat rat tot cho kinh doanh va chinh tri.

---

### 3.7. THIEN PHU (Am Tho - Tai Tinh & Quyen Tinh)
Chu: Tai loc, uy quyen

Mieu (Dan, Than, Ty, Ngo): Nhan hau, tu thien, gia tang quyen loc
Vuong (Thin, Tuat): Kha tot
Dac (Ti, Hoi, Mui): Cuu giai benh tat, tai uong

Dac biet: Thien Phu che duoc Kinh, Da, Hoa, Linh. Ky Tuan, Triet + Khong Kiep -> kho tang bi pha vo, hao tan.

CAP TU VI - THIEN PHU: Hai sao quan trong nhat la so. Tu Vi la vua, Thien Phu la than giu kho. Luon doi chieu qua truc Dan-Than.

---

### 3.8. THAI AM (Am Thuy - Phu Tinh)
Chu: Dien trach
Bieu tuong: Mat trang, nguoi me, ba noi, vo.

Mieu (Dau, Tuat, Hoi): Khoan hoa, nhan hau, nhieu nha dat
Vuong (Than, Ty): Tot dep
Ham (Dan, Mao, Thin, Ti, Ngo): Phat tan, suy bai

Quy tac: Sinh ban dem + Am Nam -> tang ve ruc ro. Ham dia gap Tuan/Triet -> lai thanh sang sua.

---

### 3.9. THAM LANG (Am Thuy - Hung Tinh & Dam Tinh)
Chu: Hoa phuc, uy quyen

Mieu (Suu, Mui): Da duc, ua tuu sac -> chu uy quyen, tai loc
Vuong (Thin, Tuat): Kha tot
Dac (Dan, Than): Co the phat huy
Ham (Ti, Hoi, Ty, Ngo, Mao, Dau): Dam, tham, hung bao, hay xuat the

Quy tac 30 tuoi: Tham Lang o Suu/Mui gap Vu Khuc -> thieu nien bat phat, ngoai 30 moi giau sang. O Tu Mo gap Hoa/Linh -> giau sang quyen chuc.

---

### 3.10. CU MON (Am Thuy - Am Tinh)
Chu: Ngon ngu, thi phi

Mieu (Mao, Dau): Nhan hau, loi danh thep, uy quyen tai loc
Vuong (Ty, Ngo, Dan): Kha tot - "ngoc giau trong da"
Ham (Thin, Tuat, Suu, Mui, Ti): Gian tra, thi phi, kien cao

Dac biet: Cu Mon o Ty/Ngo giong "ngoc trong da" -> can gap Tuan/Triet hoac Hoa Loc thi ngoc moi lo ra. Ham + Hoa Khoa -> khong dang lo ve su chang lanh.

---

### 3.11. THIEN TUONG (Duong Thuy - Quyen Tinh & Dung Tinh)
Chu: Quan loc, phuc thien

Mieu (Dan, Than): Don hau, can dam, cong danh tai loc
Vuong (Thin, Tuat, Ty, Ngo): Tot dep
Ham (Mao, Dau): Giam uy luc

Nguy hiem: Thien Tuong + Kinh Duong dong cung -> "dao ke tren dau". Gap Tuan/Triet -> ong tuong mat dau, mat het quyen luc.

---

### 3.12. THIEN LUONG (Am Moc - Tho Tinh & Am Tinh)
Chu: Phu mau, phuc tho

Mieu (Ngo, Thin, Tuat): Nhan hau, muu co, giai tru benh tat
Vuong (Ty, Mao, Dan, Than): Kha tot
Ham (Dau, Ti, Hoi): Hay nham lan, ham choi

Cap dep: Thien Dong + Thien Luong o Dan/Than -> vua giau vua tho. Thien Co + Thien Luong o Tuat -> tay chinh tri, muu binh hung.

---

### 3.13. THAT SAT (Duong Kim - Quyen Tinh & Dung Tinh)
Chu: Uy vu, sat phat

Mieu (Dan, Than, Ty, Ngo): Can dam, dung manh, quyen uy
Vuong (Ti, Hoi): Kha tot
Ham (Mao, Dau, Thin, Tuat): Hung bao, lieu linh, hay gay tai hoa

Voi Tu Vi: That Sat + Tu Vi -> vua mang kiem bau (tang uy quyen).
TU SAT O TI/HOI -> NHAT TRIEU PHU QUY SONG TOAN.

---

### 3.14. PHA QUAN (Am Thuy - Hung Tinh & Hao Tinh)
Chu: Phu the, hao tan

Mieu (Ty, Ngo): Can dam, dung manh, cuong mui
Vuong (Suu, Mui): Kha tot
Ham (Mao, Dau, Dan, Than, Ti, Hoi): Hung bao, gian ac, pha tan

Cuu van: Gap Thien Luong, Thien Hy hoac Loc -> che bot tinh hung. Gap Hao -> cang pha tan, bai hoai.

## 9. TRIET LY NHAN SINH TU 3 CUON SACH

### 9.1. Tu Jung - Synchronicity

"Khong co gi la thuan tuy ngau nhien. Moi 'trung hop co y nghia' deu mang thong diep tu cau truc sau hon cua thuc tai."

4 tang thuc tai: Khong gian <-> Thoi gian <-> Nhan qua <-> Synchronicity

Khi archetyp bi kich hoat (qua cam xuc manh, khung hoang, thien dinh...), synchronistic phenomena xuat hien nhieu hon. Day la nen tang ly giai tai sao nguoi dang trong trang thai tam linh dac biet "bat duoc" thong diep tu I Ching hay Tu Vi chinh xac hon.

### 9.2. Tu Tu Vi Dau So

"Tu Vi Dau So la de biet minh va biet nguoi - khong phai de van xin than thanh hay dot vang ma." — Van Dang Thai Thu Lang

5 nguyen tac ung dung:
1. Biet manh -> phat huy so truong (sao Mieu/Vuong = thien phu)
2. Biet yeu -> phong tranh thoi diem nguy hiem (sao Ham dia + Dai Han xau)
3. Biet thoi -> hanh dong dung luc (Dai Han tot = thoi co, Dai Han xau = an)
4. Biet nguoi -> hieu dac tinh nguoi xung quanh qua la so
5. Biet menh -> chap nhan gioi han, tap trung vao dieu co the thay doi

### 9.3. Tu Tu Vi Thuc Hanh

"Tu vi la mot khoa hoc thuc su de giai thich su tuong quan giua cuoc song con nguoi trong vu tru, chu khong phai la giua con nguoi voi ma quy, than thanh."

Vu tru quan Dong-Tay hoi tu:
- Pythagore: Tinh tu thien than lien he den con nguoi qua khi nong/lanh (am duong)
- Dong Trong Thu: Ngu hanh, am duong chi phoi van vat ke ca con nguoi
- Jung: Moi sao = mot nang luong vu tru cu the co anh huong den cuoc doi
- Tu Vi: Moi sao = mot nguyen mau (archetype) duoc chieu len vu tru
